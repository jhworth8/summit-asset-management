# Content review for the CCO

Prepared November 6, 2026, for review before the new site is published.

**Standard used:** every factual statement on the new site comes from either
the current www.summitassetmanagement.com (captured August 12, 2026, and
re-checked unchanged on November 6, 2026) or the firm's Form ADV Part 2A
(March 2026). New headings, button labels and captions are descriptive only:
no performance claims, no promises, no superlatives, no frequency claims.
All wording lives in `redesign/src/content/` and can be edited in the site editor (EDITING.md).

## Removed: wording that was not true or not supported

An earlier AI rewrite had added material that appears nowhere in the firm's
site or Form ADV. All of it has been removed:

- **Investment process:** "Continuous Daily Oversight", "Active
  Loss-Harvesting", "Systematic tax-loss harvesting reports", "Transparent
  quarterly portfolio reviews", "Stress-testing against market scenarios",
  "Direct Principal Access", "Low Institutional Expense", "Custom Volatility
  Bands", "Downside Mitigation", "Uncorrelated Diversification", "Growth
  Engine". Form ADV Item 13 says portfolios are *reviewed at least quarterly*
  and investments are *monitored on an ongoing basis*. The site now says only that.
- **Holdings:** "Dividend Growth Leaders", "strong balance sheets",
  "Factor-Tilted Smart Beta", "Private Real Estate & Credit", "US Treasury
  Notes & TIPS", "individual fixed-income ladders". Replaced with the original
  list ("may include" mutual funds, ETFs, individual securities, alternatives).
- **Planning:** "Social Security optimization", "Roth conversion analysis",
  "tax-loss harvesting", "Zero Conflicting Advice", "Central Quarterback",
  "eliminate costly coverage gaps", "maximize community impact and tax savings".
- **Peggy Adler's biography was fabricated** (AutoZone, a nonprofit,
  Vanderbilt MBA, Emory BA). Restored to the real bio: Leader Federal Bank /
  Union Planters, Magna Bank, Tulane BS, University of Memphis MBA.
- "Galactic Peggy" page and portrait.
- "Memphis and nationwide" (search description), "no proprietary products, no
  sales quotas", "Summit will not ask you to send passwords by email".
- **Terms of Service** had invented legal text. Restored to the firm's own terms.
- **Client Access** had lost the actual portal links and the required Schwab
  disclaimer. All three are restored.
- **Photo of the 2011 award plaques** ("Memphis Wealth Managers", "Top RIA
  Ranking") was the homepage hero image and one of the principle panels.
  Third-party ratings need dated disclosures under the Marketing Rule, so it
  has been replaced with a photo of Clark Centre.
- An unused "allocation" graphic with made-up model-portfolio percentages was
  deleted from the code.
