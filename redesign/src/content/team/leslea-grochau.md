Leslea joined Summit in 2023 as Client Services Administrator. Leslea focuses on all areas of client services and operations. Prior to joining Summit, Leslea worked at DHG/Forvis in their NEAR Tax and Aviation group as well as twenty-plus years in Retirement Services at MassMutual and First Mercantile.

Leslea graduated from the University of Mississippi with a Bachelor of Accountancy. She is a former member of the Board of Trustees of The Bodine School, the Board of Directors of the Junior League of Memphis and Delta Gamma House Corporation.
